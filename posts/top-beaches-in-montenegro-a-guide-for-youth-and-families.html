Top Beaches in Montenegro: A Guide for Youth and Families

Jessica Taylor 31.03.2026 • Reading time: 6 min

The Ultimate Guide: Best Beaches in Montenegro for Youth and Families

Montenegro, a hidden gem of the Balkans, has rapidly evolved into one of Europe's most sought-after summer destinations. With its dramatic limestone cliffs, crystal-clear Adriatic waters, and charming medieval coastal towns, it offers a diverse landscape that caters to both high-energy travelers and those seeking a serene family retreat. When planning your trip, finding the best beaches in Montenegro for youth and families is key to ensuring a memorable experience, whether you are looking for vibrant beach clubs or shallow, kid-friendly shores.

For those traveling with little ones, you may want to refresh your knowledge on the ultimate packing list for traveling with kids to ensure you have all the necessary gear for a comfortable beach day. Meanwhile, youth travelers often prioritize locations with nearby nightlife, water sports, and social atmospheres. This guide breaks down the top spots to help you navigate the Montenegrin coastline with ease.

Top Beaches for Youth: Energy, Music, and Adventure

If you are looking for a vibrant atmosphere where the music plays until the sun goes down and the social scene is buzzing, the Budva Riviera is your best bet. Beaches here are characterized by their proximity to trendy bars and clubs. Mogren Beach, accessible via a scenic cliffside path, is a favorite for young travelers who want a mix of adventure and style. It is tucked beneath towering cliffs and offers a perfect blend of swimming and photo-worthy views.

Another hotspot is Ploče Beach, located just outside Budva. This is not your typical sandy beach; it is a sprawling concrete and pebble complex featuring multiple swimming pools, foam parties, and high-energy music. It is essentially a club on the sea. For those planning their journey, remember that how to find cheap flights is the first step in maximizing your budget so you can spend more on beach-side cocktails and excursions.

Best Beaches in Montenegro for Families

Families require a different set of amenities: shallow entries, soft sand, and lifeguard services. The crown jewel for families is undoubtedly Velika Plaža in Ulcinj. Stretching over 12 kilometers, this "Long Beach" is famous for its fine, volcanic sand that is perfect for building sandcastles. The shallow water extends far into the sea, providing a safe environment for toddlers and younger children.

"Montenegro’s coastline offers a unique dichotomy: the rugged, party-centric shores of the north and the expansive, golden sands of the south, making it a versatile destination for any type of traveler."

Another excellent choice for families is Bečići Beach. It is cleaner and slightly more laid-back than the main Budva beaches, offering plenty of space for families to set up their umbrellas. With numerous water parks and family-friendly resorts nearby, it is a convenient hub for parents who want to minimize travel time between their accommodation and the water.

Tips for a Seamless Montenegrin Beach Vacation

  • Water Shoes are Essential: Many of the most beautiful coves in Montenegro are pebble-based. Protect your feet to enjoy the experience fully.
  • Go Early: During July and August, the most popular spots fill up by 10:00 AM. Arriving early guarantees a good spot and cooler temperatures.
  • Sun Protection: The Adriatic sun is stronger than it looks. Always carry high-SPF sunscreen, especially for children.
  • Rent Sunbeds: In places like Budva and Sveti Stefan, renting a sunbed is often worth the cost to secure a private area and an umbrella.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the beaches in Montenegro sandy or pebbly?
It depends on the region. The northern coast (Budva, Kotor) is mostly pebble and concrete, while the southern part of the country, near Ulcinj, features extensive sandy beaches.
Is it expensive to rent sunbeds?
Prices vary by location and season. On average, expect to pay between 10 and 30 Euros for a pair of sunbeds and an umbrella in popular tourist areas.
Which beach is best for a quiet family vacation?
Perazića Do or the smaller coves near Petrovac are generally quieter and more suitable for families who prefer to avoid the crowds of central Budva.
Can I find water sports for teenagers?
Yes, most major beaches like Bečići and Velika Plaža offer jet skiing, parasailing, and banana boat rides during the peak season.
